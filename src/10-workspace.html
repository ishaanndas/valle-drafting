Speaking Engagement Agreement

Whitfield Speaks LLC  ·  Cascade Health Summit, Inc.  ·  14 April 2026, Portland, Oregon

1Definitions

1.7

“Capture” means any audio, video, photographic, or transcribed record of the Presentation, in whole or in part, in any medium now known or later devised, including recordings made by attendees.VL-REC-04

1.8

“Internal Replay” means access to the Capture by registered attendees of the Event through the Host’s credentialed platform, without download, embed, or public link.

6Fee and Expenses

6.1

The Host shall pay a speaking fee of USD 28,500, of which 50% is due on execution and the balance within fifteen (15) days of the Presentation. Travel, lodging for two (2) nights, and ground transportation are reimbursed at cost against receipts.Rider ¶ 6

7Recording, Likeness, and Replay

7.1

All right, title, and interest in the Capture vests in the Speaker upon creation. The Host acquires no ownership interest in the Capture and shall make no derivative work from it except as expressly permitted below.Northwind § 8.1

7.2

The Speaker grants the Host a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to make the Capture available for Internal Replay for ninety (90) days following the Event. The Host shall delete all copies within ten (10) business days of expiry and certify deletion in writing on request.Northwind § 8.3

7.3

The Host shall not publish, broadcast, syndicate, or make publicly available any portion of the Capture, and shall not permit sponsors or third parties to do so, without the Speaker’s prior written consent, which may be withheld in the Speaker’s sole discretion.VL-REC-04

7.4

The Host may use the Speaker’s name, likeness, and approved biography solely to promote the Event, for the period beginning on execution and ending thirty (30) days after the Event. All other uses require separate written consent.

8Cancellation and Force Majeure

8.2

If the Host cancels within thirty (30) days of the Event, the full fee is payable. If the Speaker cancels due to illness, injury, or a death in the immediate family, the Speaker shall use reasonable efforts to propose a mutually acceptable substitute date within twelve (12) months and no further liability attaches.VL-FM-02

Rebecca A. Whitfield, for Whitfield Speaks LLC
Authorized signatory, Cascade Health Summit, Inc.
